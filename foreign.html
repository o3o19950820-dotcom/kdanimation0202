Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreign customers visit the salon?

Yes. You can review this guide and send a private inquiry before your visit.

Your preferred style, current hair condition, previous color or perm history, and the type of service you are considering.

No. Customer inquiry content is not displayed publicly. You need the ID and password used when submitting the inquiry to reopen it.
